catalogue `apctt-model-catalog-v1`, status **verified**, updated 2026-10-13

Generated from `contracts/model-catalog.json` (sha256 `b665a6a4ed11392b746d1f73340763c1969c6d12f715c34056cfa45d720e50c3`) by `python tools/model_catalog.py --markdown`; do not edit by hand.

These are the organizer's recommended public models for the **reference GPU: RTX 3070 Laptop GPU 8 GiB of the event machine, Asus TUF Dash F15 FX516PR-HN002 (D40, D76)**, the GPU of the event machine with 8 GB VRAM (`gpu_budget.reference_gpu`; decisions D40, D64 and D67). The organizer provides every model offline and read-only in the model store of each evaluation machine (the worker finds it in `APCTT_MODEL_STORE`). Only these models and weights your team trained (including fine-tunes and adapters of a catalogue model, named by `base_model`) may be used; a public model outside the catalogue cannot be used: each evaluation machine has one shared read-only model store, which cannot hold per-team models for this many teams, and public weights may not be shipped in your zip (decision D80). The files of a catalogue model are never in your zip: `tools/make_submission.py` refuses any file with the bytes of a catalogue weight file (the sha256 values below), however it is named or declared.

**VRAM figures are estimates** until the organizer runs `tools/hq/check_models_gpu.py` on the event GPU (`gpu_budget.measured`: false). Each estimate is weights + KV cache or activations + overhead = peak, at most the target of 7.0 GiB of the 8.0 GiB card: LLMs at 8,192 tokens of context (batch 1), encoders at a batch of 32 x 512 tokens, with 1.0 GiB of overhead for the CUDA context and temporary buffers.

**4-bit entries (`smollm3-3b-nf4`, `granite-4.0-micro-nf4`, `qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-nf4`, `mistral-7b-instruct-v0.3-nf4`, `granite-3.3-8b-instruct-nf4`) are conditional on the GPU measurement** (`measured`: false). They load the publisher's bf16 weights in 4 bits with bitsandbytes on a CUDA GPU; none of them runs on the CPU. The organizer runs `tools/hq/check_models_gpu.py` on the RTX 3070 Laptop GPU 8 GiB of the event machine, Asus TUF Dash F15 FX516PR-HN002 (D40, D76) before the kit is published and removes any entry that does not load within the budget: peak at most 7.0 GiB and a load well inside the 3-minute limit of a case (decision D41: the limit includes loading the model).

**Cold-load warning, `mistral-7b-instruct-v0.3-nf4`:** every case streams 14.50 GB of bf16 weights from the SSD and quantises them on the GPU before the first token, inside the 3-minute limit (D41). The event machine has 16 GB of RAM (D76), which cannot keep these weights in the page cache between cases, so no case starts warm. Time the load on your machine first, keep the generation short, and have a smaller model as a fallback.

## Catalogue notes

- The organizer's recommended public models for the reference GPU of the event machine, an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 Laptop GPU with 8 GB VRAM (8 GiB, decision D40): decision D64 (2026-10-10, "drop the proposal document for the environment, and select and provide public models that fit on an 8 GB GPU" (translated from Korean)) and the organizer's answer of the same day "recommend public models, use an RTX 3070 laptop gpu (8 gb vram)" (translated from Korean). HQ provides every model offline and read-only in the model store (APCTT_MODEL_STORE) of each evaluation machine, and teams download the same files for development with tools/get_models.py. Only these models and team-trained weights (including fine-tunes and adapters of these models, named by base_model) may be used. A public model outside the catalogue cannot be used: each evaluation machine has one shared read-only model store, which cannot hold per-team models for this many teams, and public weights may not be shipped in a submission (organizer answer of 2026-10-13, decision D80).
- 4-bit options (decision D67; the organizer's answers of 2026-10-10: first "add only Qwen3-4B" (translated from Korean), then "let's have several other options" (translated from Korean)): bitsandbytes 0.50.2 is in the common base from apctt-base-9e7fe1324bb6, and five LLMs that do not fit the budget in bf16 are offered in bitsandbytes nf4, loaded from the publishers' bf16 safetensors: smollm3-3b-nf4, granite-4.0-micro-nf4, qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-nf4, mistral-7b-instruct-v0.3-nf4 and granite-3.3-8b-instruct-nf4. They need a CUDA GPU (none of them is a CPU option), carry measured: false and stay in the catalogue only if HQ's GPU measurement confirms them (see the VRAM note).
- The 18 bf16 and float32 entries: every entry was checked against the Hugging Face API on 2026-10-10: licence Apache-2.0 or MIT in the card metadata, not gated, official publisher repository, revision = main at that date, safetensors weights, no auto_map in config.json (trust_remote_code false), architecture loaded by transformers 5.19.0 / sentence-transformers 5.7.0, and no match in the D53 deny list.
- The 18 bf16 and float32 entries were also re-verified independently on 2026-10-10 against the Hugging Face API (licence metadata, LICENSE files, card text, gated flag, publisher, revision = main, file sizes, no auto_map in config.json or tokenizer_config.json, no custom sentence-transformers modules), the D53/D64 deny and name rules of license-policy 0.3.0, recomputed VRAM figures, and offline CPU loads of five entries with the dev base.
- The five 4-bit entries: every entry was checked against the Hugging Face API on 2026-10-10: Apache-2.0 in the card metadata with no added restrictions in the card text, not gated (files downloaded without a token), original publisher, revision = main at that date, safetensors, no auto_map in config.json or tokenizer_config.json, model_type loaded by transformers 5.19.0, no match in the license-policy 0.3.0 model deny rules.
- VRAM figures are estimates, not GPU measurements (gpu_budget.measured is false). LLM peak = weights + bf16 KV cache for 8,192 tokens at batch 1 + 1.0 GiB overhead; encoder peak = weights + activations for 32 x 512 tokens assuming eager attention + 1.0 GiB. 4-bit entries: Linear layers at nf4 with double quantisation (0.515869 bytes per parameter), embeddings, untied lm_head and norms in bf16, + bf16 KV cache for 8,192 tokens at batch 1, + 1.0 GiB overhead. The overhead has to hold the CUDA context and the transient activations of the 8,192-token prefill; a meta-device simulation of the transformers 5.19.0 forward (checked against the MPS allocator) puts those activations beyond the KV cache at 0.61-0.91 GiB for these five entries, so mistral-7b-instruct-v0.3-nf4 (about 6.1-6.3 GiB) and granite-3.3-8b-instruct-nf4 (about 6.6-6.9 GiB) may measure above their estimate while staying within 7.0 GiB. 'bitsandbytes-nf4' here means nf4 with double quantisation and bf16 compute, and HQ measures that recipe. HQ runs tools/hq/check_models_gpu.py on the RTX 3070 Laptop GPU before the kit is published and removes any entry over 7.0 GiB or whose load is not well inside the 180-second limit.
- files lists only what loading needs, plus the repository LICENSE where there is one; download_bytes is the sum of their sizes: 23 models, 78,998,212,954 bytes (73.57 GiB), of which 27,109,610,846 bytes for the 18 bf16 and float32 entries and 51,888,602,108 bytes for the five 4-bit entries. 18 of the 23 repositories (all but the five Qwen LLM repositories) have no LICENSE file: the store and the team download tool add the licence text (Apache-2.0 or MIT) next to the files. The store holds the bf16 safetensors of the 4-bit entries, so every case that uses one reads them in full and quantises them on the GPU before the first token (D41: the 180-second limit includes the load).
- Left out after verification: Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct in nf4 (licence and files fine, but its 8,192-token prefill needs about 1.09 GiB of activations beyond the KV cache, so HQ's measurement is expected at about 7.0-7.3 GiB, above the 7.0 GiB target) and every candidate that fits the 7.0 GiB target neither in bf16 nor in nf4. Larger bf16 models do not fit at 8,192 tokens: SmolLM3-3B 7.29 GiB, granite-4.0-micro 7.96 GiB, Qwen3-4B 9.62 GiB, which is why those three are offered in nf4.
